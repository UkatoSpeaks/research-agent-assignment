# ResearchPilot Report

**Goal:** Produce a short competitive‑landscape brief comparing Notion and Obsidian for note‑taking, using public information.
**Status:** completed

## Summary
Notion and Obsidian address different note‑taking needs in 2026. Notion excels in cloud‑based collaboration, structured databases, and enterprise adoption, with a clear paid pricing model. Obsidian shines for individual users who value local‑first storage, Markdown simplicity, speed, and a rich plugin ecosystem, offering a free core and optional paid add‑ons. Market data shows Notion dominates the broader business market, while Obsidian enjoys strong developer and privacy‑focused adoption. Pricing, performance, and feature focus make each tool better suited to distinct workflows.

## Findings
### Core Architecture & Data Model
Notion is a cloud‑first, all‑in‑one workspace that stores content as blocks in a proprietary format and emphasizes real‑time collaboration and structured databases. Obsidian is a local‑first, Markdown‑based knowledge‑management tool that stores notes as plain‑text files on the user’s device, prioritising speed, privacy and extensibility via plugins.

Sources: https://tech-insider.org/notion-vs-obsidian-2026

### Collaboration & Team Features
Notion offers built‑in real‑time collaboration, shared workspaces, and extensive integration with tools like Slack and Google Drive, making it popular with teams (estimated 70 % of Fortune 500 teams). Obsidian’s collaboration is limited to optional paid Sync and Publish services; its core app is designed for individual use, with community‑driven plugins adding limited team functionality.

Sources: https://tech-insider.org/notion-vs-obsidian-2026

### Performance & Offline Capability
Mobile startup speed tests show Obsidian loads faster than Notion (iOS: 1.1 s vs 2.3 s; Android: 1.4 s vs 3.8 s). Notion is adding offline features, but Obsidian remains fully functional offline by default because notes reside locally.

Sources: https://tech-insider.org/notion-vs-obsidian-2026

### Pricing Comparison (2026)
Notion’s paid plans start at $10 per user per month (annual billing) with a free tier for individuals; AI add‑on costs $8 per member per month. Obsidian’s core app is free; optional Sync costs $4–$8 per user per month (annual discount), Publish $8–$10 per site per month, Catalyst $25 one‑time, and a voluntary commercial license $50 per user per year. For a team of 10, Notion would cost roughly $100 per month (plus AI if used), whereas Obsidian’s baseline cost could be $0 if Sync is not required, or $40–$80 per month with Sync.

Sources: https://thedigitalprojectmanager.com/tools/notion-pricing, https://checkthat.ai/brands/obsidian/pricing

### Market Position & Adoption (2026)
Notion holds about 25 % of the note‑taking software market and powers over 70 % of Fortune 500 teams. Obsidian’s active user base grew 22 % YoY to roughly 1.5 million users, with 31 % of developers in the 2026 Stack Overflow survey preferring Obsidian over Notion’s 24 %. Both tools are converging (Notion improving offline, Obsidian adding collaboration), but they still serve distinct primary use cases.

Sources: https://tech-insider.org/notion-vs-obsidian-2026
